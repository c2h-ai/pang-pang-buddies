Nearest game: Party Animals; this one differs in: the four animals never fight each other, they ride one spinning record that a DJ character scripts on the beat (count-ins, fake-outs, tilts, opened rail gates), each survives with a single gauge-limited skill of its own (the trunk on the rail sheltering friends, a short flight that carries a friend, suckers that walk, a tail that snatches), and a thrown animal hangs from the rim to be pulled up by a friend rather than being knocked out.
